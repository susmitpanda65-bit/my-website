02 / lessons from flight

When seconds matter.

These real events shaped how engineers think about materials, detection, escape, and crew response in enclosed spacecraft.

CASE FILE · 1967

Apollo 1

A ground test in a 100% oxygen cabin became a fast-moving flash fire. A difficult hatch and combustible cabin materials made escape impossible for the crew.

DESIGN · ATMOSPHERE · ESCAPEREAD CASE ↗
CASE FILE · 1970

Apollo 13

An oxygen tank failure ended the lunar landing attempt. Earlier Apollo wiring lessons helped prevent electrical arcing as the cold, damp Command Module was powered back up.

REDUNDANCY · RECOVERYREAD CASE ↗
CASE FILE · 1997

Fire aboard Mir

A solid-fuel oxygen generator ignited, spreading flame and smoke through a crowded station module. Crew teamwork extinguished it; the event informed later station operations.

DETECTION · RESPONSE · AIRREAD CASE ↗
↗

Learning compounds. Apollo 1 led to controlled combustible materials and a faster-opening hatch. Apollo 13’s redesigned wiring helped protect the crew during an emergency return. Each incident becomes a design question for the next mission.

03 / research to readiness

Turn flame science into crew safety.

A spacecraft is a small, sealed environment. NASA studies how fire starts, grows, and can be detected or put out before it threatens a mission.

04 / technology that travels

Space research, Earth-ready ideas.

NASA technology transfer has also contributed to practical tools for firefighters and early fire detection on Earth.

◉

Firefighter air tanks

A NASA and Scott Aviation collaboration described a lighter breathing-air backpack, high-pressure composite construction, improved mask visibility, and a personalized low-air warning.

NASA SPINOFF · 1976 ↗
⌁

Earlier fire detection

A detector developed for Shuttle orbiters sampled air for combustion particles and distinguished them from dust, aiming to flag a fire condition before visible flame or smoke.

NASA SPINOFF · 1978 ↗
△

Protective fire attire

Project FIRES adapted lightweight, heat-protective materials and design ideas derived from Apollo spacesuits and spacecraft for firefighter protective gear.

NASA SPINOFF · 1982 ↗
